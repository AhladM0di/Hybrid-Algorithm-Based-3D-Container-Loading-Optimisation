# Code of Conduct

Participation in this project's issues, pull requests and discussions must
follow the [Qiskit Code of Conduct](https://qisk.it/coc). Be respectful and
constructive, welcome people of all backgrounds, and do not engage in harassment.

For project-specific concerns, contact the maintainer privately at
ahlad08@vt.edu (the contact recorded in the repository's commit history).
The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation.
Use the reporting channels in Qiskit's policy for incidents in Qiskit spaces.
